## 3. `<section id="reads">` を置き換える（Marketplace の README と同じ中身に）

```html
<section id="reads">
  <h2>What it reads, and not yet</h2>
  <p><b>Printed (engraved) music</b>: staves, clefs, key and time signatures, notes and chords,
  rests, dots, accidentals, beams, tuplets, ties and slurs, grace notes, several voices on a
  staff, piano systems and groups of staves (the braces and brackets that join them), multi-bar
  rests, percent repeats, octave lines, guitar and bass TAB, drums; dynamics, hairpins, pedal
  marks, articulations and ornaments, trill lines (“tr” and a wave), arpeggios, glissandi;
  common words of expression in italic (“dolce”, “rit.”, “a tempo”, “sempre p”) and “rit.” or
  “accel.” with a dashed line; repeat bar lines, the endings with their numbers, segno and coda,
  “To Coda”, “Fine”, “D.C. al Fine”, “D.S. al Coda” and the like, written as the file's
  <code>form</code>; the tempo marking, the title, subtitle, composer and poet over the first
  system, and the rehearsal marks in boxes over the staff, which become the sections of the file.
  Best results come from clean digital pages (PDF or image exports of LilyPond, MuseScore, Dorico,
  Finale) and good black-and-white scans at about 300 dpi.</p>
  <p><b>Not yet, while in beta</b>:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Lyrics and chord names are passed over — not misread as notes, but not written.</li>
    <li>A “D.S.” or “D.C.” standing alone, and a “Fine” among other words, are not read as part of
    the form.</li>
    <li>Words of expression are written only when they are among the common ones the reader knows
    (“dolce”, “espress.”, “rit.”, “pizz.”, “sempre legato” and the like) and printed in italic;
    others (“tenderly”, “sul G”) are passed over. A “rit.” with a dashed line too short to show a
    dash is written as plain text.</li>
    <li>A rehearsal mark of a single letter in a box becomes a nameless section; section names
    printed without a box are not read.</li>
    <li>Lead sheets: the melody, its header and marks are read over the chord diagrams and verses,
    and the one-line rhythm staff under the melody is written as slashes; the chord names and
    diagrams are passed over. Parts laid out in columns are read poorly.</li>
    <li>Handwritten music is not read. Colour or low-contrast scans, and pages saved small (under
    about 100 dpi), read worse than black-and-white scans at 300 dpi.</li>
    <li>Words — title, tempo, rehearsal marks — are read with the text recognition built into
    Windows, in the languages installed for it, so a Latin title on a Japanese-only PC may come
    out with a wrong letter or two.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Check what it reads: a mark says where it was unsure, but a bar with no mark can still be
  wrong. The reader's own page on the Marketplace keeps this list current; where it and this page
  disagree, it is right.</p>
</section>
```

## 4. `site/videos/SHOTLIST.md` に足す 2 本

表に:

```
| 16 | `scan-read.mp4` | scan — Read a scan | なし | 約 25 秒 |
| 17 | `scan-transform.mp4` | scan — Check what it read | なし | 約 20 秒 |
```

手順:

```markdown
### 16. `scan-read` — PDF を右クリックしてから、最初の印を直すまで
素材は `scan-import.png` と同じ Tchaikovsky op. 39 no. 5（1904 年 Schirmer、IMSLP、パブリックドメイン）の
PDF 1 ページ。Lily# と Scan Import を入れた VS Code、フォルダにその PDF だけ。
1. エクスプローラーで PDF を右クリック → *Convert Image/PDF to Lily# (.lys)…*。ページの選択はそのまま OK。
2. 右下の進み具合の通知を見せる（数秒）。`.lys` が開き、プレビューが右に出る。赤い印が 2 つ見える所まで
   スクロール。
3. エディタのタイトルバーの絵のボタン → *Show Original Scan*。スキャンがプレビューの横に開き、同じ小節に
   赤い破線の枠。
4. プレビューの赤い印をクリック → エディタがその小節へ飛ぶ。`@todo(… "a quarter short of 2/4 …")` を読ませる
   （1 秒止める）。
5. 足りない音を打ち、`@todo(…)` を消す。赤が消える。終わり。
前後は切る。打鍵は Screencast Mode で見せる。

### 17. `scan-transform` — 読んだ楽譜を AI に移調させる
16 の続きのファイル（印を直した後）。GitHub Copilot か別のモデル提供元にサインイン済み。
1. 右手の 1〜2 小節目を選択。`Ctrl+I`、*transpose up a fourth* と打って Enter。
2. 元の横に彫られた候補が出るのを待つ。After/Before を 1 回切り替える。
3. Accept。ファイルが変わり、プレビューも変わる。終わり。
待ち時間が長ければ切る。
```
