# Contributing

Thank you for taking an interest in Lily# Scan Import.

## Reports are the most useful help

The reader gets better from pages it reads wrongly. When it misreads a page, please open an
issue with:

- the page image or PDF (only one you have the right to share: your own engraving, a public
  domain print, or a page under a free license, with its source),
- what the reader wrote and what the page actually says (a bar number is enough),
- the extension's version (shown in VS Code's Extensions view).

## Pull requests

Code contributions need a Contributor License Agreement (CLA), which is not set up yet. Until it
is, please open an issue describing the change instead of a pull request; the maintainer will
make the change and credit you in the commit.

## Data

Do not add printed music that is still under copyright, or files whose license does not allow
redistribution. Every file under `data/` is listed in [data/LICENSES.md](data/LICENSES.md) with
its source and license.
